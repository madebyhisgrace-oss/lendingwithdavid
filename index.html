Told you don't qualify elsewhere? These are built for unique income, assets, and situations — most with no tax returns required.

NonQM

Bank Statement Loans

Qualify on 12–24 months of bank deposits instead of tax returns. Built for self-employed borrowers whose taxable income doesn't reflect real earnings.

Self-employedNo tax returnsTo $4M

DSCR Loans

Qualify on the property's cash flow, not personal income. If the rent covers the payment, you generally qualify. Built for investors scaling a portfolio.

Commercial inquiries are referred to Valere Commercial, an affiliated commercial lender that independently conducts its own underwriting and renders all commercial credit decisions. Business-purpose loans are made to entities for residential investment property — not owner-occupied homes or second homes, and not office, retail, warehouse, industrial, group homes, assisted living, daycare, or SBA lending. Programs generally expect experienced investors with comparable completed projects. Availability, terms, and eligibility vary by state, product, and borrower — ask me for current guidelines before you count on any of it.
